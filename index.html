The Five Memory Tiers

Box 1 · Daily Interval: 1 Day

Newly logged slips. Reviewed daily until initial recall and orthography stabilize.

Box 2 · 3 Days Interval: 3 Days

Early consolidation. Verifies retention after immediate short-term memory decays.

Box 3 · Weekly Interval: 7 Days

Intermediate recall. Tests recognition across varied reading sessions and contexts.

Box 4 · Fortnightly Interval: 14 Days

Long-range retention. Confirms deep familiarization before advancing to final graduation.

Box 5 · Mastered Interval: 30 Days

Permanent reading vocabulary. Fully incorporated into long-term comprehension with light monthly maintenance.
